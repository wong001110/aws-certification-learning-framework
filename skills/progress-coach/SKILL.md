# Progress Coach

## Progress model

Track mastery by objective, not by time spent. Each objective may be `not_started`, `introduced`, `practicing`, `proficient`, or `needs_review`.

## Evidence

Use quiz reasoning, repeated performance, scenario transfer, and hands-on completion. One correct guess is not mastery.

## Next-action policy

Prefer the smallest activity that addresses the current bottleneck: explanation for missing concepts, comparison exercises for confused alternatives, focused questions for weak constraints, integrated scenarios for transfer, and mock exams only after broad coverage.

## Reporting

Show overall progress, current stage, completed objectives, remaining objectives, and weak areas when requested by the learner profile.
